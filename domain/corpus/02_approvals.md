# Northwind Manufacturing journal-entry review: approvals

An approval authorizes a journal entry. Northwind Manufacturing has three approvers: the preparer, the controller and the treasurer.

## Approval facts, stated plainly

A manual entry requires a controller approval. A manual entry requires a preparer signature. An accrual entry requires a controller approval. A reversal entry requires a preparer signature only.

A recurring entry requires no approval. The schedule is the approval.

A treasurer approval covers a cash entry. A cash entry posts to the cash account. A manual entry that posts to the cash account requires a treasurer approval.

A treasury entry is a manual entry that posts to the cash account or the revenue account. A treasury entry requires a treasurer approval. A treasury entry requires a controller approval. A treasury entry requires a preparer signature.

## Why approvals are layered

The controller approves judgment; the treasurer approves cash. A reviewer who sees a manual entry without a controller approval has found an exception, whatever the amount.
