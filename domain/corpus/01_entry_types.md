# Northwind Manufacturing journal-entry review: entry types

> Illustrative. Northwind Manufacturing, its ledger and its approvers are invented so the vertical is legible.

A journal entry posts to the ledger. A journal entry names an account. A journal entry carries an amount and a period.

Northwind Manufacturing books four entry types: the manual entry, the recurring entry, the accrual entry and the reversal entry.

## Entry facts, stated plainly

A manual entry originates from a person. A recurring entry originates from a schedule. An accrual entry recognizes an expense before the invoice. A reversal entry reverses an accrual entry.

The manual entry carries a narrative. The accrual entry carries a narrative. The reversal entry references an accrual entry.

## Why the types are separate

A schedule cannot choose; a person can. That difference is the whole reason journal-entry review exists.
