# Northwind Manufacturing journal-entry review: accounts and exceptions

The ledger carries the revenue account, the expense account, the cash account and the suspense account.

## Account facts, stated plainly

A suspense entry posts to the suspense account. A suspense entry requires a reconciliation. A reconciliation clears the suspense account before the close.

A revenue entry posts to the revenue account. A manual entry that posts to the revenue account requires a controller approval and a treasurer approval.

## Exception facts, stated plainly

A missing approval creates an exception. A suspense balance at the close creates an exception. A round amount on a manual entry creates a review flag. A review flag is not an exception.

## Why exceptions are written down

The reviewer decides from the entry type, the account and the approvals, and from nothing else.
